Free schema.org JSON-LD partials for Hugo rental/property sites — unit-level and property-level structured data, generalized beyond just apartments.

## Short blurb (Reddit r/gohugo, Hugo Discourse, r/SEO, etc.)

If you're running a Hugo site for any kind of rental property (apartments,
single-family rentals, storage, vacation rentals) and haven't added
structured data yet, here are two small partials that'll do it for you —
one for an individual listing/unit, one for the property/business as a
whole. Both are generalized so you can override the schema.org `@type` to
match your actual business instead of being locked into "apartment
complex." Free, MIT licensed, no theme dependency.
